### **Academic and English Requirements for Canadian Institutions**

Most Canadian universities and colleges have the following minimum entry requirements for Nepali applicants:

- **Minimum academic score:** 2.8 CGPA or 50 percent or higher in your most recent qualification

- **IELTS Academic:** Overall band 6.5 with no individual band below 6.0

- **PTE Academic:** Overall score of 60 with no band below 60

Some institutions and specific programs—especially engineering, health sciences, and business may require higher academic scores or have prerequisite subjects such as mathematics or biology. It is important to verify the exact requirements for your chosen course before applying.

### **Complete Document Checklist for Canada Study Permit from Nepal**

Having your documents ready and correctly organised is one of the most important steps. The following is the standard checklist for Nepali applicants:

- Valid passport

- All academic documents: SEE/SLC, High School (+2), Bachelor degree, and Masters if applicable

- Updated CV or resume and **detailed Statement of Purpose (SOP)**

- Two to three recommendation letters

- **Proof of funds:** GIC for **CAD 22,895** and education loan documents

- **Income evidence:** Tax clearance certificates of your financial sponsors

- **Letter of Acceptance (LOA) and Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL)**

- Medical examination results and Passport-sized photographs

- Canadian immigration forms including IMM 1294, IMM 5645E, and IMM 5707
