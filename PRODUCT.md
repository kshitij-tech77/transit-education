# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Nepali students (18–28) making a high-stakes decision about studying abroad — often first in family to consider it. Parents frequently co-decide. They arrive with mix of excitement and anxiety, uncertain about costs, visas, and which country fits their goals. Secondary surface: consultancy staff managing content via the CMS admin.

## Product Purpose

Transit Education is Nepal's study-abroad consultancy. It guides students from "I want to study abroad" through visa approval, connecting them to universities in Canada, Australia, UK, USA, Germany, Ireland, Italy, New Zealand, and South Korea. Success means a student walks in undecided and leaves with a clear path — and eventually a visa.

## Brand Personality

Ambitious, Modern, Empowering. The site should make a Nepali student feel that their global future is within reach, that Transit has the expertise to get them there, and that choosing the right consultancy is the first real step toward it.

## Anti-references

Generic ed-tech: stock photos of diverse students jumping, rainbow color schemes, cluttered feature grids, low-contrast text on busy backgrounds, layouts that feel like a government form or a brochure from 2012.

## Design Principles

1. **Aspiration over anxiety** — Students are making a life-changing call. Every screen should make them feel capable and excited, not overwhelmed by process.
2. **Expert confidence** — No hedging copy, no generic templates. Transit should look like it's done this 10,000 times, because it has.
3. **Show, don't tell** — Stats, success stories, visa approvals, and real destination guides over marketing superlatives.
4. **Earned trust** — Specificity earns trust. Real numbers, real office locations, real counsellor names — not generic testimonial carousels.
5. **Purposeful crimson** — The brand color carries authority and energy. Use it as a signal, not wallpaper; let white space do the heavy lifting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Priority on mobile — target users are Nepali students primarily on phones. Ensure text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body, ≥ 3:1 for large text. Keyboard navigable. Screen reader labels on icons and form fields.
